# S5 — Roadmap Re-validation: Is "Blender-DNA" the Right Target, or AI Over-Engineering?

_Principal-architect cold verdict, 2026-06-09. Theme: critically re-validate the Blender-DNA architecture roadmap (`architecture-roadmap-blender-2026-06-04.md`). Strongest case FOR, strongest case AGAINST, then endorse / modify / reject. Every claim is grounded in an A_ (first-hand X-GIS code finding) or B* (cited external authority) file; nothing is asserted that those files do not support.*

---

## 1. COLD VERDICT (1 paragraph)

**MODIFY — keep ~4 of the roadmap's increments, rename the whole thing, and reject the "Blender-DNA" framing outright as a category error.** The roadmap is _not_ sustainable as authored for a 5yr / 3D-tiles / 4D goal, but not because its destination is wrong — it is wrong because (a) the literal Blender model it invokes solves problems X-GIS does not have, and (b) the increments that carry the actual debt-reduction (god-file decomposition, duplication deletion) are 0% executed while the project pivoted into producing audits _about_ the plan. B1 is decisive on (a): X-GIS is a stateless-per-session streaming view in TypeScript over already-standardized external formats (PMTiles/MVT/GeoJSON/style-JSON), so Blender's DNA serialization, ID-library-linking, RNA introspection, automatic operator-undo, and global `bContext` are native-C-app workarounds with _no web payoff_ — and several are debt Blender's own maintainers want to shed (B1 §2). What genuinely transfers is a short list of _patterns, not Blender code_: dependency-scoped invalidation (B1 §3 PARTIAL), an immutable per-frame snapshot, SoA hot loops, and a thin command/event bus — and three of the roadmap's four named authorities (DirtyDomains, OperatorBus-as-thin-bus, projections-table) sit inside that defensible subset (B1 §5). The fatal problem is execution and over-decomposition, proven by A5: of 21 increments, the easy/inert ~13 shipped (~505 LOC of substrate) while _zero_ of S17/S18/S19/S20 — every increment that deletes duplication or decomposes a god-file — ran, and `map.ts` actually _grew_ 604 lines (+21%) partly because of this roadmap's own dispatch-alongside wiring (A5 §2.2). A roadmap whose stated purpose is shrinking god-files, that made the worst god-file bigger and produced 12 research docs in one day instead of its 6 hard increments, has stalled at exactly the part that justified writing it.

## 2. ROOT CAUSES (not symptoms)

The symptom is "the roadmap didn't finish." The root causes are four, in priority order:

**RC1 — Wrong North Star: the analogy was chosen for grandeur, not fit.** The single closest peer to X-GIS is MapLibre (B3: _same_ language, _same_ runtime, _same_ problem — tiled vector data → GPU), yet the roadmap is named after Blender — a native C/C++ offline-capable authoring tool with a persistent user document (B1 §preamble). B3 §0 shows MapLibre already solved X-GIS's exact problems with four orthogonal authorities (declarative style spec, Source/Layer split, Actor/Dispatcher worker boundary, dirty-flag render loop), and B3 §5 explicitly maps MapLibre's three dirty flags (`_styleDirty`/`_sourcesDirty`/`_placementDirty`) onto X-GIS's named `DirtyDomains`. Choosing the _farther_ analogy when the _nearer_ one is structurally identical is the originating error — it imported Blender's vocabulary (DNA/RNA, operator-undo, depsgraph engine) and with it a temptation toward machinery X-GIS doesn't need. A5 §4.3 independently flags the "DNA/RNA … Blender-DNA" framing as "borrowed grandeur" and "post-hoc narration over two small commits."

**RC2 — Over-decomposition manufactured the illusion of progress.** A5 §4.2 shows 21 increments where ~8 real units exist; the fine-graining is "defensible-on-paper (each matrix-gated) but it manufactures 21 ceremony-bearing steps." B5 §(b) gives the principle: over-abstraction is the _dominant_ SOLID failure, and the cure is "named seams with concrete collaborators + Rule of Three," not an increment/interface per concern. The 21-step granularity _correlated with_ the failure: it let the project ship the easy 13 and call it progress (A5 §4.2, §5). This is the owner's stated fear — speculative abstraction — realized in the _plan_ itself.

**RC3 — The "5 authorities" are taxonomy theater where they're not real.** A5 §2.3 measures only ~1.8 of 5 authorities as real in code: A1 projections-table (~1.0, genuinely flipped for world-copy routing per A1 §3.2), A5 shader-DSL (1.0 but "we didn't touch the good part" is not progress), A2 OperatorBus (~0.4 — it is a 27-line _side-log_, not the `applyOp→DirtyDomains` mutation funnel it was specified as; A2 §2 confirms it is "wired but inert"), A3 DirtyDomains (~0.4 — A2 §2 proves 7 of 8 domains are write-only, exactly one consumer), A4 EvaluatedTile (0.0 — does not exist). B2 §7's meta-lesson is the discriminator: longevity comes from _one repeated move_ (extract a fat concern into a self-registering, lifetime-scoped unit and invert the dependency), not from a 6-box ASCII authority diagram. Naming a thing an "authority" before building it is the inversion of that lesson.

**RC4 — Research-to-execution ratio is inverted, and the hard increment is parked behind "CI can't see it."** A5 §3.1–3.3: three audit sessions, ~500 LOC shipped, `MEMORY.md` over its size cap. The increments stopped _exactly_ at the risk boundary — everything shipped is inert/camera-local; everything unshipped is god-file decomposition (S20) or cross-package deletion (S19). A4 §3 proves _why_ S20 is genuinely scary and parked: of 262 runtime tests, **260 are pure CPU math, 1 touches a GPU, 0 call `.render()`**; the real-GPU matrix gate is **local-only and manual** (A4 §3b), and it is **blind to S20's highest-risk surface** — A5 §4.5 notes flat synthetic fills render 56.75% under SwiftShader while the _same geometry extruded_ renders 0.00%, i.e. the gate the roadmap trusts to make S20 safe cannot see the extrusion path S20 must preserve. So the safety net that was supposed to make the plan "aggressive" cannot certify the one increment that matters, and producing audits is lower-risk than attempting it.

## 3. TARGET STATE (what "good" looks like, grounded in what the references actually do)

"Good" is **MapLibre's structure, named in MapLibre's terms, decomposed via Branch-by-Abstraction, with only the genuinely-transferable Blender/Unreal/deck.gl patterns layered in.** Concretely:

- **One authority per concern, contracts not call-graphs (B3 §10).** The style spec is the _sole_ declarative authority; everything downstream is derived. B3 §1 is explicit: X-GIS's recurring bugs trace to having _multiple_ authorities (style JSON, IR, projections-table, shader literals) that drift — A1 §3.2 confirms the PROJECTIONS flip is only ~40% done (capability predicates still hand-coded as `projType === 1 || === 2 || === 6` at camera.ts:982/1064). Good = the table is the SoT for _capability membership_ too, not just world-copy routing.

- **Dirty-flag render loop with per-domain flags + a convergence `idle` event (B3 §5, B4 §2.1).** This is the _one_ Blender idea that genuinely transfers (B1 §3: "the _pattern_ transfers; the _implementation_ does not — build a small fixed dirty-domain system, not a general depsgraph engine"). MapLibre proves three named flags + a single `_update()` funnel + "fire idle and stop" is sufficient at 100k+ LOC for a decade (B3 §5). deck.gl/three.js add the coalescing-flag discipline and the "every async arrival must re-invalidate" invariant (B4 §2.2, recommendation 4). Good = `DirtyDomains` becomes _consuming_ (today A2 §2: 7/8 write-only), drives granular skips, and fires `idle`.

- **Single owner per concern, god-files decomposed along MapLibre's seams (B3 §2, §10; B2 §2).** VTR (A1: 5,440 LOC, a 2,054-line `render()` method) splits into MapLibre's three-way `Bucket`(geom→buffer) / `WorkerTile`(orchestration) / `ProgramConfiguration`(buffer→shader) — B3 §11 says "adopt the split, not a monolith." Unreal's subsystem pattern (B2 §2) supplies the lifetime-scope taxonomy (device / map-session / frame) that answers "where does this state live" — directly curing A2's god-object finding (XGISMap, 3,431 LOC, ~10 responsibilities, 4 redundant camera caches).

- **An immutable per-frame snapshot the GPU reads + framework-owned resource teardown (B1 §5, B4 recommendation 2).** Not Blender COW (B1 §3: WeakMap/COW is a native-thread workaround; JS gets thread-safety free from the Worker structured-clone boundary). deck.gl's `finalizeState` (framework destroys resources on diff-removal) is strictly better than three.js's caller-driven `dispose()` (B4 §11 REJECT) — and answers A3's monotonic glyph-atlas leak + worker-pool teardown gaps.

- **A thin typed command/event bus + plugin registry for 3D-tiles/4D extensibility (B1 §3 PARTIAL, B2 §5, B3 §4).** B2 §5's one-way dependency rule (feature→core, never core→feature; register don't reference) is the highest-leverage pattern _after_ subsystems for the unnamed-future capabilities (3D-tiles source, temporal layer). A6 AXIS β confirms the gap is real: grep for `registerLayer|CustomLayer|registerSource` returns no registration surface — to add 3D-tiles today you fork the engine. Keep OperatorBus _thin_ (B1 §3: the `wmOperator`/undo machinery does NOT transfer — a map has no document to undo).

- **A four-oracle test matrix on a real GPU (B5 §d, A4 §3).** The "compiles + unit-green ≠ renders right" gap is a _structural_ property of graphics testing (B5 §d, citing the shader-compiler oracle-problem literature), not a discipline failure fixable with more unit tests. Good = property-based invariants (CI) + golden-master image MATRIX on a real-GPU lane + metamorphic relations (pan-and-back, world-copy) + differential vs d3-geo. A GPU-enabled CI runner is "the lasting unlock" (A4 §4) that converts the local-only matrix into an actual gate — and is the prerequisite that makes S20 safe to attempt.

**What "good" explicitly does NOT include** (B1 §5 reject-list, confirmed): a bespoke "DNA" file format, an ID-datablock+library-linking document model, an RNA-style introspection layer, COW datablocks, automatic operator undo, a global `bContext`. None solve an X-GIS problem; all import Blender's costs/known-debt.

## 4. RECOMMENDATIONS (each with rationale, evidence, risk, blast-radius)

### R1 — REBRAND: kill "Blender-DNA," rename to "Invalidation + Decomposition (MapLibre-shaped)." Drop the 5-authority/21-increment formalism to ~6 plain units.

- **Rationale:** The name is a category error that biases toward native-app machinery (B1 §5: "should be renamed and rescoped"), and A5 §4.1–4.2 shows the formalism manufactured 21 ceremony steps over ~8 real units, which _hid_ that only the easy ones shipped. The nearest peer (MapLibre, B3) already names every needed authority correctly. B5 §(b) Rule-of-Three: earn each abstraction with ≥3 real consumers or a current bug class.
- **Evidence:** B1 §5; B3 §11 (per-pattern adopt/adapt table); A5 §4.1, §4.2; B5 §(b).
- **Risk:** LOW — pure renaming/rescoping; no code change. Risk is _political_ (the prior session is invested in the Blender narrative).
- **Blast-radius:** Documentation only (the roadmap doc, MEMORY index). Zero source files. But it unblocks honest prioritization.

### R2 — DO S19 FIRST, mechanically, before any new design doc. Delete the 3 copies of quantize-ecef into `@xgis/shared`.

- **Rationale:** A5 §2.4 + A1 §3 + A6: the CPU↔GPU drift bug-class root (ranked #1 debt) is `quantizeAxis`/`tileEcefCenterFromMerc` duplicated across vector-tile-renderer, synthetic-earth-surface-backend, and compiler/vector-tiler — still 3 copies. B1 §4 anti-pattern #4 (DOD: behavior/data separation, shared kernels) and B5 §(b) (the PROJECTIONS-table abstraction _earns_ its existence at 7+ projections past Rule-of-Three) both support consolidating genuine duplication. A5 §5 Q4: forbid a new `.md` until S19 is committed.
- **Evidence:** A5 §2.4, §5; A1 §3 (gpu↔projection cross-import seam = where drift bugs live); B5 §(b).
- **Risk:** LOW-MEDIUM — mechanical extraction; but the Windows autocrlf polygon-variant-diff gotcha (per MEMORY) and the byte-equal drift gates mean the move must keep emitted bytes identical. Branch-by-Abstraction (B5 §c) applies: one shared kernel behind the same call sites.
- **Blast-radius:** ~3 source files + their test/helper copies; closes the #1 drift bug-class. Low logic risk, high debt-payoff per LOC.

### R3 — REPLACE the local-only manual matrix with a GPU-enabled CI runner BEFORE attempting S20. Make S20-safety the actual next increment.

- **Rationale:** A4 §3b/§4 names a GPU CI runner "the lasting unlock"; without it the raster regression class is "mitigated only procedurally — i.e. by human discipline, which fails under deadline pressure." A5 §4.5 proves the current gate is blind to S20's extrusion path (extruded geometry → 0.00% under SwiftShader). A5 §5 Q1: "if the matrix can't see the extrusion path, what _would_ make S20 safe — and why isn't building THAT the next increment instead of more audits?" B5 §d: the four-oracle real-GPU matrix is the _correct_ characterization test for a renderer; golden-master-before-refactor is mandatory (B5 §c Strangler/BbA).
- **Evidence:** A4 §3a–3d, §4; A5 §4.5, §5; B5 §c, §d.
- **Risk:** MEDIUM — requires CI infra (a GPU-equipped runner; cost + setup). But it is the _precondition_ that makes the highest-risk increment (S20) attemptable at all — skipping it means S20 stays permanently parked.
- **Blast-radius:** CI config + the matrix harness wiring; no production source. Unblocks the entire decomposition payload.

### R4 — DECOMPOSE the god-files via Branch-by-Abstraction along MapLibre seams + Unreal subsystem scopes. This IS S20, done safely.

- **Rationale:** A1/A2/A4 converge: VTR 5,440 LOC with a 2,054-line `render()`, label-pass 1,065 LOC with a ~980-line `execute()`, XGISMap 3,431 LOC / ~10 responsibilities. B3 §2/§11 gives the target seams (Bucket/WorkerTile/ProgramConfiguration); B2 §2 gives the lifetime-scope cure for the god-object (device/map/frame subsystems with mandatory `initialize(deps)`/`deinitialize()` — also fixes A3's worker-pool + glyph-atlas teardown leaks and A6 E1's missing device-loss recovery surface). B4 §1 gives the reactive layer-descriptor + `finalizeState` model so resource lifetime is framework-owned. B5 §c mandates the _method_: golden-master first, Branch-by-Abstraction seam, migrate one consumer at a time, each commit revertible — never edit the 5,440-line VTR in place (use Sprout).
- **Evidence:** A1 §2.1–2.4, §3.1 (the map↔render-loop import cycle + 43 host reach-throughs); A4 §2b–2c; B2 §2; B3 §2, §11; B4 §1–2; B5 §c.
- **Risk:** HIGH — this is the dangerous increment (S20: arena GPU UAF ordering, A5 §3.2; the S16 staleness hotfix `c2ca9842` already proved the risk model right). Strictly gated on R3 (GPU CI) landing first.
- **Blast-radius:** The largest — VTR, render-loop, map.ts, label-pass, the import cycle (A1 §3.1). This is _the_ payload; do it last, behind the safety net, incrementally.

### R5 — MAKE DirtyDomains consume (finish S14/S16/S17/S18) and unify the 4 redundant camera caches, modeled on MapLibre's three flags.

- **Rationale:** A2 §2 is damning: the dirty/op subsystem is "wired but inert" — 7/8 domains write-only, double-bookkeeping on every setter that gates nothing, presenting a _false_ "domain-routed invalidation" affordance over a legacy boolean. A2 §1 finds 4 redundant camera-state caches with no shared generation counter (the manual-memoization desync trap). B3 §5 is the proven fix: split the single coarse dirty bit into named domains so each stage skips cheaply and "re-arm" bugs go away because the landing callback sets the _specific_ domain. B4 §2.2 adds the async-arrival invariant.
- **Evidence:** A2 §1, §2, §6 (risks 1–2); B3 §5; B4 §2.2, recommendation 4.
- **Risk:** MEDIUM — behavior-changing (each domain-consumer is a real skip path); matrix-gated. But far less dangerous than R4, and it _retires_ the inert overhead rather than shipping it to v1 (A2 §2: "indefensible if it ships to v1 in this state").
- **Blast-radius:** map.ts (the setters + `shouldRenderThisFrame`), render-loop, the per-pass `consume` sites. Medium; pays down A2's #1–#2 risks and the thermal cost (per MEMORY, adaptive-DPR dead code).

### R6 — ADOPT the Evented mixin + `map.on('error')` + a thin registration API (cheap, high-value, NOT in the roadmap at all).

- **Rationale:** These are MapLibre/Unreal/deck.gl patterns the Blender roadmap _omits entirely_, yet they are flagged ship-blockers. B3 §4: the `Evented` mixin + bubbling chain + error-special-case is "~200 lines of pure TypeScript, zero GPU coupling, near-free." A6 E2: there is no `'error'` event — device loss, validation errors, worker crashes reach `console.error` but never surface observably (CRITICAL). A2 §3: `easeTo`/`flyTo` are `jumpTo` stubs that break the move-event cadence. B2 §5 / A6 AXIS β: a `registerSourceType`/`registerLayerType` surface is the prerequisite for 3D-tiles without forking the engine.
- **Evidence:** B3 §4; A6 E2, AXIS β; A2 §3; B2 §5.
- **Risk:** LOW — additive public API, no render-path change for the event/error work; the registration API is more involved but B2 §5 confirms "a registry object + an index.ts is enough" (no plugin-asset machinery).
- **Blast-radius:** layer.ts (event union), map.ts (event plumbing), a new registry module. Low; closes A6's two CRITICAL observability gaps and the extensibility gap.

## 5. EXPLICIT UNCERTAINTIES / DISAGREEMENTS

1. **Does the roadmap deserve "modify" or "reject"?** I land on MODIFY, but it is close to a _reject-the-framing, salvage-the-parts_ split decision. A5's author argues "not wrong, just unfinished in the way that matters" (A5 §5). B1's author is harsher: "largely a category error, should be renamed and rescoped" (B1 §5). I weight B1 higher on the _naming/scope_ and A5 higher on the _execution_ diagnosis — they are compatible, and the synthesis is MODIFY. A reasonable reviewer who weights the sunk narrative cost differently could call it REJECT-and-rewrite. The disagreement is about labels, not substance: both agree the transferable subset is ~the same 4 patterns and the god-file decomposition is the unexecuted core.

2. **Is OperatorBus worth keeping at all?** A2 §2 + A5 §2.3 show it is an inert side-log today (double-bookkeeping that gates nothing). B1 §3 says a _thin_ command bus PARTIAL-transfers (the API-surface need is real; the undo machinery is not). I recommend keeping it _thin_ (R6's command/event bus), but I cannot rule out that the honest move is to **delete it** until a real consumer exists (B5 §(b): no abstraction without ≥3 consumers). Uncertainty: whether the bounded op-log (`MAX_LOG=256`) ever earns its keep for easeTo-as-ops, or is permanent overhead. A5 §4.4 notes the "human-call" gate for this was unnecessary (the log was built without it).

3. **4D/temporal is under-specified by every source.** A6 AXIS ε is the only first-hand evidence: X-GIS has a time _cursor_ (animation clock, `TimeStop`) but no time _axis on features_ (no `valid_time`/`epoch` in the data path). B3 §2 (4D note) and B5 §(a) treat temporal as an _additive_ source dimension — but both are INFERENCE, not cited features of the references. **I cannot validate the roadmap against the 4D goal** because neither the roadmap nor the B-references concretely specify what 4D-city rendering requires beyond "a time coordinate on data." This is a genuine gap: the roadmap was authored for invalidation/decomposition, not 4D, and re-validating it against a 4D target it never targeted is partly unfair.

4. **The Blender depsgraph analogy is _partially_ legitimate and I want to be fair to it.** A5 §1.5 and §4 (final "genuinely good" list) and B1 §3 (depsgraph row = PARTIAL transfer) agree the _core diagnosis_ is correct and well-sourced: a monolithic `_needsRender` boolean structurally hides under-invalidation bugs (the S16 staleness bug `c2ca9842` is a real instance). My "reject the framing" is about the _DNA-serialization/operator-undo/RNA_ parts, NOT the depsgraph-invalidation insight — that insight is real and is exactly what R5 finishes. The disagreement with the roadmap is scope (it bundled a good idea with native-app baggage and grand naming), not the kernel.

5. **Whether R3 (GPU CI runner) is achievable is outside my evidence.** A4 §4 asserts it is "the lasting unlock" but does not establish cost/feasibility of a GPU-equipped CI runner for this project's CI provider. If it is infeasible, R4 (S20) has no safe path and the honest conclusion shifts toward "park the god-file decomposition indefinitely and ship the cheap wins (R1/R2/R5/R6) only" — a materially different roadmap. This is the load-bearing unknown for the whole "modify" verdict.

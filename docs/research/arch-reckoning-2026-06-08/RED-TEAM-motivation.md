# RED-TEAM: Motivated-Reasoning + Stall Lens on the Arch-Reckoning Synthesis (S1–S6)

*Hostile review, 2026-06-09. I read all of S1–S6 and cross-checked their load-bearing facts against the A* evidence and the live tree. The synthesis's **facts are accurate** — I verified VTR=5440, map.ts=3431, label-pass=1065, operator-bus.ts=27 LOC (a genuine side-log: `dispatch()` only logs+tags, the setter mutates separately), dirty.ts has exactly **one** production `consume()` consumer (`label-pass.ts:271`), and `render-loop.ts:36` is a real **value** import of `XGISMap` (used at lines 597/610/611), so the `map↔render-loop` cycle is genuine, not type-only. The numbers are not where this synthesis is weak.*

*Where it is weak is the **motivation and stall layer** — the exact thing this review was commissioned to find. Below, every challenge names the severity and what the integrator MUST address or concede.*

---

## BLOCKING

### B1 — The synthesis is itself the 13th–18th audit document in the audit-instead-of-execute pattern it condemns. This is the central self-refutation and it is never confronted.

Every S-file diagnoses the same root: "research-to-execution ratio is inverted… planning is the comfortable activity and S20 is the uncomfortable one" (S1 R4/§4.104; S5 RC4; S6 R3; A5 §3.3). I verified the git log: the **last 12 commits on this branch are 100% `docs(research)`** — zero feat/fix since `c2ca9842` (2026-06-08). The total non-doc source delta of the entire `claude/invalidation-perf-phase-*` branch vs main is **383 insertions** (label-pass/icon-stage/text-stage S16 family). **S1–S6 are six MORE research documents produced in that same window.** The synthesis condemns producing audits as "lower-risk dopamine than attempting [S20]" (S6 R4) while *being itself* a ~3,000-line cluster of new audits that ships zero lines of the S19/S20 work it calls the keystone.

This is not a minor irony — it is a **structural validity problem**. A synthesis whose own #1 recommendation is "stop writing .md files until S19 is committed" (S5 R2; A5 §5 Q4: "the next session should be forbidden from writing a new `.md` until S19 is committed") and which then **adds six .md files** has falsified its own thesis by existing. The integrator MUST either (a) concede that S1–S6 themselves violate the freeze they prescribe and commit to the freeze taking effect *now* (no more synthesis docs, S19 next), or (b) explain why this round of auditing was the exception — and "the owner asked for a red-team" is not a sufficient answer, because the owner also paid for the *previous* audit rounds. **Concede: the safety-net/audit work has become the stall, and these documents are part of it.**

### B2 — "Build the safety net first" is presented as a genuine unlock, but the synthesis never proves the GPU-CI runner is procurable — and if it isn't, the ENTIRE sequence has no safe path and collapses to "park S20 forever." This is a stall-in-disguise risk the synthesis admits in fine print and then ignores in its conclusions.

Every S-file makes Phase-0 (GPU-enabled CI golden-master gate) the precondition for everything that matters: S4 R1 ("the unlock both audits name"), S5 R3, S6 Phase 0 ("gates everything else… the highest-leverage dollar"). **All six then bury the load-bearing caveat in §5:**
- S4 §5.3: "Cost/feasibility of GPU CI is **asserted, not costed**… the single largest open feasibility question and R1's risk rating depends on it."
- S5 §5.5: "Whether R3 (GPU CI runner) is achievable is **outside my evidence**… If it is infeasible, R4 (S20) has no safe path."
- S6 §5: "Is a GPU-enabled CI runner actually procurable/affordable?… **the biggest open risk to the whole sequence and should be priced first.**"

This is a **motivated-reasoning tell**: the synthesis builds an entire confident 5-phase migration sequence on a foundation it concedes it cannot verify, then files the unverified foundation under "uncertainties" so the body can read as decisive. If GPU-CI is infeasible (cost, WebGPU-headless-on-hosted-runner support, flake), then by the synthesis's own logic: S20 stays parked, the god-files keep growing, and "build the net first" becomes the permanent reason nothing structural ever ships — **the exact stall pattern, now blessed as strategy.** The integrator MUST: (1) treat GPU-CI procurement as a hard **spike with a go/no-go date** *before* endorsing the sequence, not a Phase-0 assumption; (2) write the explicit fallback branch — what happens to S20 if the spike returns "infeasible"? "Scheduled real-GPU lane on a dev box with a human merge gate" (S6 §5) is named but the synthesis already called that "not a gate" (A4 §3b). **The synthesis cannot have it both ways: either the manual real-GPU run IS a viable gate (then GPU-CI is not load-bearing and S20 could be attempted now), or it is NOT (then the whole sequence is hostage to an unpriced procurement).** This contradiction is unresolved and it is the spine of the plan.

### B3 — "Lock golden-masters on a 2,054-line method, THEN decompose" is circular and the synthesis half-knows it. A golden master over a fused god-method pins WRONG-but-consistent behavior — and the matrix is ALREADY 26/45 known-broken.

S4 R3 is the thesis operationalized: "Lock the behavior of VTR's `render()` (~2,054 LOC)… as real-GPU golden masters first; then decomposition becomes safe." But S4 §5.2 immediately concedes the defeater: "a 2,054-LOC `render()` may have output so entangled that a coarse golden baseline locks in *wrong-but-consistent* behavior (the matrix already documents 26 known-broken cells)." A4 §3c confirms **26 of 45 matrix cells are `expected_red`** (known-broken). So the proposal is: *lock the current behavior of the renderer — including its 26 documented bugs — as the gold standard, then refactor to preserve it.* That doesn't make the refactor safe; it makes the refactor **bug-preserving by construction**, and it cannot distinguish "I preserved behavior" from "I preserved the bug." The escape hatch offered (metamorphic relations, R5) is real but the synthesis rates it "low-medium" confidence and does not show it covers the extrusion/OIT paths that S20 actually touches.

Worse, this is **circular with B2**: the golden master must run on real-GPU CI (it doesn't exist), AND the matrix is blind to the one surface S20 must preserve — "flat fill renders 56.75% under SwiftShader, the same geometry extruded renders **0.00%**" (S4 §3 / A5 §4.5). So the synthesis's own evidence says: the gate you must build first cannot see the thing you must protect. The integrator MUST concede that "golden-master-first" is **necessary but demonstrably not sufficient** for the god-methods in question, and MUST specify what *new* extrusion/OIT matrix cells must exist and pass on real hardware before S20 is even attemptable — otherwise "build the net first" is an infinite-regress yak-shave (build CI → build extrusion oracle → build OIT oracle → … → never decompose).

## MAJOR

### M1 — The synthesis trivializes-and-patches the SAME sin it accuses the roadmap of: it manufactures 6 RECs per theme × 6 themes (~30+ recommendations) where the real action list is ~5 items, and the redundancy hides that almost everything reduces to the same 3 moves.

S1 R4 indicts the roadmap for "over-decomposition of the *plan* hid under-execution of the *work*… 21 increments where ~8 real units exist." Yet the synthesis itself produces **six near-identical REC sets** that, deduplicated, collapse to: (1) GPU-CI golden gate, (2) three enforcement lint gates, (3) S19 dedup, (4) make DirtyDomains consume, (5) decompose god-files via Branch-by-Abstraction. Every S-file re-derives this same kernel under different theme-branding (S1's "REC-1 enforcement gates" = S4's "R6" = S6's "Phase 1"; S1's "REC-2 S19" = S5's "R2" = S6's "Phase 2"; etc.). The "synthesis" is **6× restatement, not convergence-with-prioritization** — and the cross-references (every S cites every other S) create an *illusion of triangulated agreement* when in fact the six themes were handed the same A*/B* evidence and unsurprisingly reached the same conclusion. That is not independent corroboration; it is shared-prior echo. The integrator MUST collapse S1–S6 into **one** ranked action list of ~5 items with a single owner-facing sequence, and MUST stop presenting "all six themes agree" as strong evidence when the six themes are not independent.

### M2 — "Will the migration stall again?" is the literal commissioning question, and the synthesis's answer is structurally optimistic without earning it. The S16 staleness hotfix is the one data point on the team's ability to land risky invalidation work, and it CUTS AGAINST the plan.

The synthesis treats the stall as a *solved-once-we-have-the-net* problem. But the only in-window evidence of the team attempting behavior-changing invalidation work is S16 — which **shipped a bug and needed an immediate staleness hotfix** (`c2ca9842`; A5 §3.2: "S16… immediately needed a staleness hotfix, confirming the risk was real"). S16 was a *localized, single-matrix-cell* change — the easiest class of behavior change — and it still drew blood. The synthesis cites this as proof "the risk model was right" (S5 §5.4) but never draws the obvious adverse inference: **if the team cannot land the easiest behavior-change increment without a follow-up bug, the confidence that they will land S20 (GPU UAF ordering, the hardest) once a net exists is unearned.** The net catches regressions *after* they're written; it does not make the writing correct. The integrator MUST address: what evidence is there that the *execution capability* (not just the safety net) is sufficient for S20? The honest answer may be "none, and that is a second reason S20 stays parked" — which materially weakens "MODIFY/proceed" toward "this team should ship the cheap wins and not attempt S20 until execution capability is demonstrated on Phase 3."

### M3 — REC-3 ("cut the map↔render-loop cycle by passing frame-state down") is wrong on the mechanism. Removing the 43 `host.<field>` reach-throughs does NOT break the cycle, because the value import remains via static constants.

The synthesis (S1 R3, S6 R1) frames the cycle as caused by the `Pick<XGISMap>` host back-reference and prescribes replacing it with a `FrameInputs` struct. I verified the tree: `render-loop.ts` also uses `XGISMap` as a **runtime value** at `:597` (`XGISMap.FLICKER_GRACE_FRAMES`) and `:610–611` (`XGISMap.FLICKER_LOG_CAP`). These are static-class-constant reads, independent of the host-field reach-throughs. **Therefore the proposed `FrameInputs` refactor — even done perfectly — leaves the value import `import { XGISMap } from './map'` in place, and the runtime cycle survives.** This is a concrete instance of the synthesis "trivializing-and-patching": it diagnosed the cycle correctly but prescribed a fix that doesn't actually cut it. The integrator MUST add "relocate the two static constants out of `XGISMap` (to a shared constants module)" to REC-3, and MUST note that REC-1c's cycle-gate cannot go green on the host-struct work alone — confirming the synthesis's own sequencing tension (S1 §5.1) is *worse* than stated.

### M4 — The synthesis's "honest credit" for the package DAG and shader-DSL functions as motivated balance that softens the verdict. The "2/5, not a 1" framing is repeated six times and is doing rhetorical work.

Every S-file leads with "NOT sustainable, 2/5" then spends a paragraph on why it's "not a 1" (the acyclic package DAG, the shader-DSL authority, "the diagnosis itself is good"). Note that **two of the three credited assets are the team's own prior research/abstraction work**, and the third (package DAG) is real but, per the synthesis itself, sits "on top of a module-level structure that is the opposite of sustainable" (S1 §1). Crediting "the diagnosis is good" as a sustainability *asset* is circular — the diagnosis is *these very documents*. This is the **trivialize-and-patch reflex applied to the verdict itself**: the score is kept off the floor partly by counting the existence of good audits as architectural merit. The integrator MUST decide whether "we have an excellent diagnosis" belongs in a *sustainability* score at all, or whether it is exactly the comfortable-activity output that B1 identifies as the problem. A colder read is **closer to 1.5/5**: the one genuine structural asset (DAG) is undermined at the module level by the synthesis's own account, and "good diagnosis" is not load-bearing structure.

### M5 — OperatorBus / op-log: the synthesis flags "kill it or complete it?" as an open question in all six §5 sections but never resolves it, while simultaneously assuming the "one mutation funnel" target state. This is an unconfronted internal inconsistency.

S6 §3 target state assumes "the OperatorBus actually being the single mutation path." But S1 §5.5, S5 §5.2, S6 §5 all flag that the op-log may be **speculative abstraction with zero external-authority support** ("B3/B4 give no authority for an op-log/undo system in a *renderer*" — S2 §5.3) and possibly should be *deleted* per the Rule of Three. The synthesis cannot both (a) target "OperatorBus as the mutation funnel" and (b) flag that the op-log earns no keep — these are contradictory target states held simultaneously across the six docs. Given the owner's *explicit* fear of speculative abstraction, the burden is on the synthesis to take a position, and the evidence it cites points to **delete**, not complete. The integrator MUST resolve this to a single recommendation; ducking it into "uncertainties" six times is the deferral-disguised-as-thoroughness the synthesis itself condemns in the roadmap's "6 human-call gates" (A5 §4.4).

## What the integrator MUST address or concede (summary obligations)

1. **(B1)** Concede S1–S6 are part of the audit-stall; enforce the .md freeze *starting with these docs*; commit to S19 as the literal next action with no further synthesis.
2. **(B2)** Convert GPU-CI from a Phase-0 *assumption* to a time-boxed go/no-go spike; write the explicit "if infeasible" fallback; resolve the contradiction that manual real-GPU is simultaneously "the gate after we automate it" and "not a gate today."

5. **(M2)** State the team's S20 *execution-capability* evidence (the S16 hotfix cuts against it); decide whether "ship cheap wins, defer S20 until Phase 3 proves capability" is the honest verdict.
6. **(M3)** Fix REC-3: relocate `XGISMap` static constants or the cycle survives the prescribed fix.
7. **(M5)** Take ONE position on OperatorBus (evidence says delete); stop deferring it into §5 six times.

The synthesis is factually sound and the diagnosis is genuinely good. But it commits, at the meta level, the precise sins it convicts the roadmap of — over-decomposition (6× restatement), deferral-as-thoroughness (the same 3–4 questions parked in every §5), and producing comfortable analysis instead of the uncomfortable S19 commit. **Its single most honest move would be to stop, delete five of its own six recommendations as duplicates, price the GPU-CI spike, and ship the quantize-dedup. Anything else is the boulder rolling back down.**

# fill-extrusion: MapLibre vs X-GIS — why the rendered results differ on the globe (2026-07-13)

**Scope.** Answers: "MapLibre and X-GIS render 3D polygons (fill-extrusion) differently — what EXACTLY differs and why does the output differ?", focused on the GLOBE projection.

**Method / honesty.** Every X-GIS claim is code-verified with `file:line` (read on 2026-07-13, main). Every MapLibre claim is **training knowledge, marked ⚠**, assuming MapLibre GL JS v4/v5 (globe = v5 `projection: 'globe'`); pin against maplibre source before using a ⚠ claim in a fix. No GPU run was performed for this doc (read-only analysis); items needing a live A/B are listed at the end.

## 0. TL;DR comparison table

| Axis | MapLibre way ⚠ | X-GIS way (code) | User-visible consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Geometry space** | Flat-earth construct: vertices in tile-local Mercator (int16 EXTENT grid), z = height in meters scaled into Mercator-z by `pixelsPerMeter` computed at the **transform center latitude** (`mercatorZfromAltitude(1, center.lat) * worldSize`). v5 globe re-projects the same flat vertices per-frame in the VS. | True 3D: CPU bakes walls+roofs directly in **ECEF on the WGS84 ellipsoid** (`lonLatToECEF`, shared/src/ecef.ts:38-54) as a per-tile RTC residual, u16×6-quantized (polygon-mesh.ts:42-56, 279-303, 463-481). VS = dequant + DSFUN camera recentre + one linear ECEF MVP (polygon.ts:416-431, 669) | X-GIS buildings are geometrically "planted" on the curved earth at every zoom/pitch; MapLibre buildings are flat-map geometry that only *looks* curved after VS reprojection (and at city zooms usually isn't reprojected at all, see Globe row). Heights: MapLibre uses one center-lat scale for the whole frame; X-GIS uses true meters per vertex. |
| **Extrusion direction** | Flat mode: all buildings extrude along the **map-plane +z** (parallel, never converge). v5 globe: radial lift along the **sphere** normal in the VS (`spherePos * (1 + ele/R)`-style), sphere not ellipsoid. | Per-vertex along the **geodetic (ellipsoid) normal**: `lonLatToECEF(lon, lat, base+h)` displaces `h` metres along the WGS84 normal (ecef.ts:50-52; polygon-mesh.ts:381-387, 436) | Near the globe limb / at low zoom MapLibre-flat buildings all lean the same way (parallel walls); X-GIS walls converge toward the earth as physically expected. X-GIS vs MapLibre-globe differ only by sphere-vs-ellipsoid (≤ ~0.19° normal deflection — X-GIS's own note, polygon-mesh.ts:421-427). |
| **Tessellation** | Per ring edge: 4-vertex wall quad with per-edge normal packed into `a_normal_ed`; roof earcut in tile coords; **tile-boundary (clipper) edges skipped** via `isBoundaryEdge`. | Same scheme — per-edge wall quads (4 verts + 2 tris, winding-normalised, polygon-mesh.ts:308-395) + earcut roof in the Mercator plane (polygon-mesh.ts:184-216, rationale 170-174; holes supported 203-213, 439-448). **Tile-boundary synthetic edges NOT skipped** — a wall for EVERY ring edge (polygon-mesh.ts:154-163). | Nearly identical silhouettes. But X-GIS grows an interior wall at every tile seam crossing a building; invisible when opaque, visible as extra dark planes under translucency (feeds the #1080 symptom). |
| **Lighting model** | Flat per-face colour computed per-vertex from Mapbox's fixed formula (`directional = clamp(dot(n, light), 0, 1)` remapped by intensity + Rec.709 luminance, `|n.z|`-gated 0.7→0.98 vertical wall ramp, ambient +0.03); **light dir is a constant in the flat map/viewport frame**, default anchor=viewport ⇒ rotates with bearing. No shadows, no AO (Mapbox v3 AO = n/a in MapLibre). | Same formula, ported verbatim (polygon.ts:707-757: luminance 713-716, ambient 717, remap 730-737, wall ramp 743-756) — but the light is **one global ECEF vector**: Mapbox `[radius, azimuth, polar]` → (E,N,U) → rotated into ECEF by the **camera-anchor ENU basis** (#420; vector-tile-renderer.ts:4068-4095, anchor trig 3969-3976), dotted with per-vertex **ECEF face normals** (walls = horizontal outward ENU→ECEF, polygon-mesh.ts:340-377; roof = sphere-radial, 421-436). Intensity in `light_dir_ecef.w`, colour RGBA8-packed (WS-9; polygon.ts:722-729, VTR 4087-4101). | Identical look near the camera anchor (CPU-oracle-confirmed per VTR comment 4068-4076). Across the globe they diverge: X-GIS behaves like a sun (roofs/walls darken with angular distance from the anchor, and the distribution re-anchors as the camera moves); MapLibre's shading is position-independent. X-GIS also does not bearing-rotate the light (acts like anchor=map), so rotating the map changes relative wall brightness differently than MapLibre's viewport default. |
| **Depth model** | Standard perspective depth buffer, `depthRangeFor3D` sub-range; no log depth; no per-feature bias. | **Log depth**: VS rewrite `z = log2(w+1)·fc·w` + per-fragment `frag_depth` (log-depth.ts:24-37; polygon.ts:705, 826-833), plus per-feature xor-shift **depth jitter** (~±1e-5 NDC) to break shared-wall coplanar z-fights (polygon.ts:817-834), plus per-layer NDC bias 1e-3 (vector-tile-renderer.ts:4117-4124). Pipeline: `depth24plus-stencil8`, less-equal, write on (gpu-shared.ts:78-86; pipeline-factory.ts:708-722). | X-GIS keeps depth precision from orbit to street (globe engine requirement); MapLibre's linear-ish depth is tuned for map viewing distances. X-GIS deterministically resolves row-house shared walls of different colours; MapLibre coincident walls z-fight (usually invisible: same colour). |
| **Translucency** (`fill-extrusion-opacity < 1`) | Two-pass: (1) depth-only prepass, (2) colour pass gated on the prepass depth + a 3D stencil against double-blending coincident polys ⇒ only the **front-most shell** is shaded once. (The pre-2019 Mapbox offscreen-texture+composite variant is retired ⚠.) | Single pass, plain alpha blend + depth test/write into the main target (`BLEND_ALPHA`, pipeline-factory.ts:675, 708-722); the WBOIT path (McGuire-Bavoil MRT, oit-pass.ts:16-104; fs polygon.ts:949-980; pipeline 954-1019) **exists but is hard-disabled** — `isOitExtrude = false` (bucket-scheduler.ts:320-333; its "matches MapLibre" premise is contradicted by #1080). | The headline visual bug class: X-GIS shows back/interior faces through overlapping translucent buildings and double-darkens overlaps (draw-order dependent); MapLibre shows one clean translucent shell. Confirmed as issue **#1080** (open, MapLibre-parity fix pending). |
| **Base / height properties** | `fill-extrusion-height/base` fully data-driven **per-vertex paint binders**, re-evaluated (composite binders) so zoom-interpolated heights animate **every frame**; shader clamps `max(0, …)`. Data-driven `fill-extrusion-color` per feature. | Heights/bases baked **once at tile-decode** in the MVT worker: `extrude:` AST evaluated per feature with `$zoom = tileZoom` proxy (mvt-worker.ts:64-103, 414-419; extrude-eval.ts:29-35; ≤0 → dropped, extrude-eval.ts:73) → `generateWallMeshExtrudedECEF(heights, bases)` (upload-coordinator.ts:507-547). Uniform mirrors `extrude_height_m`/`extrude_base_m` are **written but read by no shader** (VTR 4129-4133, 4187-4190; polygon.ts:118, 121 declared-only — grep of every emitted WGSL shows struct-decl only) ⇒ constant-height (`kind:'constant'`) styles produce **no lift** on the tiled path (only `kind:'feature'` ASTs reach the worker, show-source-maps.ts:168-191). Extrude colour is the layer-uniform `u.fill_color` (polygon.ts:711); `fs_fill_extrude` has no variant hook (polygon.ts:991-1010) ⇒ per-feature colour not wired for extrusions. Spec rows: color/opacity/height/base/translate(+anchor)/pattern/vertical-gradient = supported, AO = unsupported (spec-coverage/paint-fill-extrusion.ts:3-54). | Zoom-interpolated heights grow smoothly in MapLibre; in X-GIS they step when new tiles decode. A Mapbox style with constant `fill-extrusion-height: 15` renders 3D in MapLibre but (currently) flat in X-GIS. Per-feature-coloured buildings (e.g. by type) are multi-colour in MapLibre, single layer colour in X-GIS. |
| **Globe behaviour** | v5 `projection:'globe'` is **adaptive**: vertical-perspective at low zoom, transitions to plain Mercator around z≈12 — i.e. at typical building zooms fill-extrusion renders with the FLAT math above; during the globe phase geometry is VS-reprojected per frame (f32), sphere model. Horizon handling is tile-level. | Globe is the native path: pre-lifted ECEF-RTC verts + camera-anchor DSFUN recentre `cam_ecef_off_h/l` (polygon.ts:138-146, 416-431 — the G7 fix note: without recentre every extruded tile collapsed to the camera tile), per-fragment **hemisphere cull** + **rim alpha fade** at the limb (polygon.ts:786-791, 1000-1004; `globe_eye` 155-161). The #599 globe fill drape explicitly **excludes** extruded layers (`currentExtrudeMode === 'none'` gate, vector-tile-renderer.ts:3127-3136) — extrusions always draw as direct ECEF geometry. Flat-projection arms lift `z_plane = wall_height·is_top` in real metres, knowingly unscaled vs the Mercator plane metric (polygon.ts:676-682, 347-348, 384). | On the actual sphere at low zoom: X-GIS buildings hug the curve, fade smoothly at the limb, and occlude correctly against the horizon; MapLibre's are subject to its transition (and pop per tile at the horizon ⚠). ## 1. X-GIS extrusion geometry path (code-verified)

1. **Style → per-feature heights.** `extrude:` / `extrude-base:` feature ASTs (from Mapbox `fill-extrusion-height/base`, converter paint-fill-extrusion.ts:105-162; IR lower-bindings-paint.ts:134-147 feature / 426-443 constant) are shipped per source-layer to the MVT worker (show-source-maps.ts:168-191 — **feature form only**). The worker evaluates them per feature at decode time (`$zoom = tileZoom`, extrude-eval.ts:29-35) into `heights` / `bases` maps (mvt-worker.ts:414-419, 484-485).
2. **Mesh bake (CPU).** `generateWallMeshExtrudedECEF` (map/src/core/polygon-mesh.ts:146-492): ring coords are absolute Mercator metres → lon/lat → **`lonLatToECEF(lon, lat, base|base+h)` on the WGS84 ellipsoid** (writeVertex 267-303) — the same geoid as the flat-fill tiler (`packECEFPolygonVertices`, doc 113-120), so walls/roofs and surrounding ground fill share one surface. Walls: one quad per non-degenerate ring edge, winding-normalised, flat per-edge outward normal built by rotating the edge tangent −90° in the local ENU horizontal plane and mapping ENU→ECEF at the edge midpoint (340-377). Roof: earcut in the Mercator plane (184-216), dedicated verts with **sphere-radial lighting normal** (421-436 — deliberate lighting-only approximation). Per-mesh symmetric quantization of the ECEF-RTC residual to 2×u16/axis after the lift (463-481). Layout = `POLYGON_EXTRUDED_FORMAT`, stride 44: q_xy/q_z + feature_id + abs_lon/abs_lat + face_normal×3 + wall_height + is_top (compiler/src/tiler/polygon-vertex-format.ts:54-63).
3. **Upload.** Extruded slices route through the mesh generator with the tile-corner **ellipsoidal** ECEF anchor (upload-coordinator.ts:507-547).
4. **Vertex shader** `vs_main_ecef_extruded` (polygon.ts:637-774): dequant (shared `dequant_ecef`, 526-534, 669) → projection ladder (310-432): globe/3D arm re-centres by `cam_ecef_off_h + cam_ecef_off_l` (the DSFUN split of `tileEcefCenter − cameraCenter`, 138-146) and applies the single ECEF MVP (416-431); flat arms lift `z_plane = wall_height × is_top` (347-348, 384). Then fill-translate (702-703), **log-depth** rewrite (705), and the MapLibre lighting block evaluated **per vertex** into `v_color` (707-771).
5. **Fragment** `fs_fill_extrude` (991-1010): passes interpolated `v_color` through after the shared discards (hemisphere cull / lat clamp / parent-clip, 786-815), multiplies the **rim fade** (1000-1004), writes pick + **log frag-depth with per-feature jitter** (817-834).
6. **Pipelines.** `fill-pipeline-extruded(+fallback)`: `vs_main_ecef_extruded` + `fs_fill_extrude`, `cullMode:'none'` (courtyard/dome interiors), depth write, `BLEND_ALPHA`, MSAA (pipeline-factory.ts:708-722, 752-766, 675, 683). Pattern-extrude twins at 809-834. OIT twin at 954-1019, scheduled never (bucket-scheduler.ts:333).

## 2. MapLibre fill-extrusion (⚠ training knowledge, GL JS v4/v5)

- Bucket: tile-local int16 vertices, per-edge wall quads with packed `a_normal_ed` normals (t-flag for top/bottom in the encoding), earcut roof, **`isBoundaryEdge` skips tile-border walls**; base/height as per-vertex data-driven paint binders with `max(0,…)` clamps in the shader.
- Painter: drawn in the translucent pass with depth ReadWrite (`depthRangeFor3D`); **opacity < 1 ⇒ the two-pass depth-prepass technique** ("Draw transparent buildings in two passes so that only the closest surface is drawn" — verbatim comment in draw_fill_extrusion), stencil (`stencilModeFor3D`) against coincident-poly double-blend.
- Lighting: `style.light` `[r, azimuth, polar]` (+90° azimuth convention) → cartesian, anchor=viewport default rotates by −bearing; the vector lives in the flat map frame; formula as ported into X-GIS (see table).
- Globe (v5): per-vertex VS reprojection onto a unit **sphere** with radial elevation lift; adaptive globe→Mercator transition (~z12) means building-scale views typically render with the flat path.
- Height scale (flat): meters → Mercator-z via `pixelsPerMeter` at the transform **center** latitude.

## 3. Why the pictures differ — ranked

1. **Translucent overlaps** (biggest reported delta): interior/back faces visible + double-darkening in X-GIS vs MapLibre's single shell (#1080; bucket-scheduler.ts:333 vs ⚠ depth-prepass). X-GIS's extra tile-seam walls (polygon-mesh.ts:154-163) add interior planes MapLibre never creates.
2. **WebGL2**: X-GIS renders no extrusions at all (#1056; vector-tile-renderer.ts:1056) — a 100% output difference on that backend.
3. **Lighting distribution on the globe**: same local look, different global behaviour (ECEF sun-like light anchored at camera centre, no viewport-anchor bearing rotation — VTR 4068-4101) vs MapLibre's position-independent screen-anchored light ⚠.
4. **Constant-height styles & data-driven colour**: flat (no lift) and single-colour extrusions in X-GIS where MapLibre shows 3D / per-feature colours (u.extrude_height_m unread; fs_fill_extrude variant-less).
5. **Zoom-interpolated heights**: stepwise (tile-decode bake, extrude-eval.ts:29-35) vs continuous ⚠.
6. **Geometry fidelity at planet scale**: X-GIS ellipsoidal ECEF + log depth + rim fade vs MapLibre flat/transitional sphere ⚠ — building lean at the limb, horizon occlusion, high-pitch stability, and (flat-mode only) the cos(lat) height-scale mismatch (polygon.ts:676-682).
7. **AA/depth micro-artifacts**: 4×MSAA + per-feature depth jitter vs no MSAA + raw z-fight on coincident walls ⚠.
